Privacy Policy

How GRIDLINE handles your information, in plain English.

The short version
  • We collect what you need to sign in, and what you type into your jobs. Nothing else.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Your account and jobs are stored in a database in London.
  • AI features only run when you press the button, from your browser, using your own Anthropic key.
  • You can download everything, and delete your account yourself from Settings.

1. Who we are

GRIDLINE is made and run by Andrew Hamilton, trading as Andrew Hamilton Engineering (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at hamo.andrew@gmail.com. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office, registration number {{ICO_REGISTRATION_NUMBER}}.

For your account, we decide how your information is used, so we are its controller. For the job records a business keeps in GRIDLINE, the position is different — see section 8.

On your deviceWhat the app keeps in your browser is set out in the cookies & device storage page.
If you email usYour email address and whatever you send.

3. What we don’t do

We don’t run analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. We don’t build profiles, sell data, or share it for anyone’s marketing. GRIDLINE makes no automated decisions about you: anything the AI or the contract radar suggests stays a suggestion until a person accepts it.

4. Why we use it (our lawful bases)

  • To provide GRIDLINE to you (contract): your account, storing and syncing your jobs, signing you in.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure; keeping the change log intact so that the record can be relied on, including in a contract dispute; answering you when you contact us.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose something.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Where a provider above is based outside the UK, the transfer is protected by the safeguards that provider offers, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Ask us if you want the detail.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account: while it exists. Removed when you delete it.
  • Projects only you belong to: until you delete them or your account.
  • Projects you share with others: for as long as the project exists, because the other members rely on the record. A claim under a construction contract can usually be brought for six years after the work, or twelve if the contract was a deed, so project records may need to be kept that long. When you delete your account, your changes in shared projects stay, but your name and account are removed from them (section 9).
  • Server logs: for our providers’ standard periods.
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to deal with them.

GRIDLINE doesn’t currently keep separate backups of the database. You can download a full backup of your own jobs at any time from Settings → Backup.

8. When your jobs contain other people’s details

Job records naturally hold other people’s information — a client’s contact, a subcontractor’s name, a photo of site. If you use GRIDLINE for a business, that business decides what goes into its records and why, so it is the controller of that information and we process it on its behalf, only to run GRIDLINE. The business is responsible for having a good reason to hold it and for telling those people, as it would for any other project record.

If someone contacts us about information in your records, we will pass it to you and help you respond. Business customers can ask us for a data processing agreement.

9. Your rights, and how to use them

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, and object to or restrict how we use it.

  • Download it: Settings → Backup → Download backup.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete my account. Projects only you belong to are deleted permanently, with their photos and drawings. Projects you share keep their record: your changes stay, shown as a deleted user, and your name, email and key are removed. This cannot be undone.
  • Anything else: email hamo.andrew@gmail.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We’d appreciate the chance to put it right first.

10. Children

GRIDLINE is a business tool for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information about children. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. The database controls access row by row, so only a project’s own members can read its records. Passwords are hashed by our sign-in provider, nostr private keys never leave your device, and the change log can’t be altered from the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affected your information, we would tell you, and the ICO where the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters to how your information is used, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
