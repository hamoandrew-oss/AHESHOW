02KNOW WHERE EVERY PACKAGE STANDS
PARTS · DRAWINGS · PROGRAMME · PROCUREMENT

Every mark, its drawing, its dates, its status — one register.

Drop in the material list and the parts register builds itself — every column, rafter, purlin and fitting with NEEDED → ORDERED → DELIVERED status and tonnage. Drop in the drawing set and every sheet files itself to its marks. The programme is a Gantt you can drag on a phone, with crews and men-per-week on the bars. So "where's package 3?" is never a phone call: it's 82% delivered, two fittings with the fabricator, erect starts Friday — and anything delayed past its erect date is on your dashboard before it bites.

Run the job — and the commercial and handover file writes itself.

Every action you take on site becomes the record a contract needs. Snags are numbered, photographed and exported as the subbie's punch list.
