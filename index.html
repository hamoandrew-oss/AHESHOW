What it does

Four things, done properly

Most site software is built for the office. GRIDLINE is built for the person running the job: the one who enters the information is the one who gets the benefit.

01

Tells you what needs you

Every job scored for health from what's actually in its registers, with a brief of the three things that matter today.

02

Shows where every package stands

Parts, drawings, programme and procurement in one register, with the 3D model coloured by status.

03

Keeps you right on the contract

NEC4 early warnings, instructions and compensation events with every clock counted, so nothing time-bars on your watch.

04

Builds the record as you go

Quality, handover, red lines and the money record fill in from the work you're already doing, ready to export.

Every section

A tour of GRIDLINE

Everything below is in the app today. Each section talks to the others: a delayed part shows on the dashboard, a failed cube offers an NCR, a note becomes an RFI.

Start here

Dashboard

Every live job on one screen, scored for health, with a plain-English brief of what needs you today.

  • Job health with the reasons behind it
  • Today: the alarms from every module in one list
  • AI brief written in site language (bring your own Anthropic key)
Jobs → Delivery

Packages, parts and programme

One register for every package: each mark, its drawing, its dates and its status.

  • Material lists import straight into a parts register
  • Not ordered · ordered · delivered · delayed
  • Drag-and-drop programme (Gantt) tied to the parts
Jobs → Drawings

Drawings and 3D model

Drawing sets file themselves to their marks, and IFC models open in the browser.

  • Revisions kept, current one on top
  • Interactive 3D model: click a part, measure, isolate
  • Parts coloured by delivery status
Jobs → Quality

Quality

A Quality overview first, then every register behind it.

  • Snags with photos and owners
  • Concrete register: pours, loads, cube results, import your site sheet
  • ITP register with the document in a sidebar and revisions
  • NCRs with costs · material testing (stone, plate load)
Jobs → Money

Commercial and applications

The money side of the job, built from the same records.

  • Applications for payment and what's owed
  • Subcontractor packages and their position
  • NCR and change costs feed the picture
Jobs → Files

Files

Every document the job needs, in folders you organise.

  • Nested, coloured folders · batch upload
  • Revisions read from the file name
  • Expiry and review dates for insurances, certificates and RAMS
NEC4

Contracts

Early warnings, instructions and compensation events with every clock counted for you.

  • Raise in one panel: what happens next, explained
  • Clause 61.3 time bar and reply clocks
  • Register: your move · waiting on them · done
  • Paperwork prepared for CEMAR
Design

RFIs and drawing review

Keep on top of design: what's open, what's late, and what's been reviewed.

  • Import ACC or BIM 360 RFI exports; sort and search
  • Chase the designer: you pick the RFIs; ACC, Aconex or BAM refs
  • Review: mark up drawings (clouds, numbered comments), send back to the detailer
Handover

Handover file

The handover record builds itself as you work, package by package.

  • Checksheets imported from ACC with status per package
  • Red line mark-up status from the ACC files log
  • Status drawing: duct runs coloured on the CAD layout from the checksheets
  • Progress and red line PDF exports
Meetings

Meetings and actions

Minutes and actions that carry forward until they're closed.

  • Actions with owners and due dates
  • Open · completed · all, with closing comments
  • Private to you, synced across your devices
Notes

Notes

A site notebook: by day and by job, with actions you can't lose.

  • Type [ ] to make an action · photos with mark-up
  • Start today from yesterday's note, open actions carried across
  • Turn a line into a snag, early warning or draft RFI · share a sketch
Tender

Tender pipeline

Bids you're pricing and the jobs you've won or lost.

  • Countdown to return date
  • Checklist, key dates and Q&A per tender
  • Win rate over the last 12 months
Subcontractors

Subcontractors

Who's doing what across your jobs, and where they're stretched.

  • Packages and open snags per subcontractor
  • Spot clashes across jobs
  • Contact details in one place
Settings

People and access

Bring your team in, with the right access.

  • Invite by email · owner, editor or viewer
  • Choose which jobs each person sees
  • Backups and data export

03Spotted for you. Possible events picked up from your own RFIs, meetings and site records, suggested and never issued without you.
