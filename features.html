JOB-001PACKAGES & PARTS
FROM PDF TO PARTS LIST

Drop in the material list. The register builds itself.

Feed GRIDLINE the Advance Steel material list PDF and every mark, section, grade, length and weight lands as a live row — grouped into packages like Steelwork or Cladding. Four taps track each part from not ordered to delivered, or set a whole package Ordered in one click when it's bought as a lot. Civils read in the same way — drainage manholes, catch pits and draw-pit schedules land with their own REF, TYPE, SIZE and DEPTH columns. The job's tonnage and delivery percentage roll up on their own.

DRAWINGS THAT FILE THEMSELVES

Any drawing set in. Every sheet finds its part.

Import your fabrication drawings — any set, any size — and GRIDLINE reads each sheet and allocates it to the part it details, filed into its package folder with revision and author. Tap a part on site and its drawing opens at exactly the right sheet; download hands over just that sheet, named and revision-stamped. No renaming files, no hunting through folders — viewing and coordination sort themselves.

JOB-006SUBCONTRACTOR COMMERCIALS & RESOURCE

Contract, PMIs, payments — and what's actually due.

THE FILE BUILDS AS YOU BUILD

Handover tracked to the pour, not the promise.

Every job carries its handover file from day one — each package seeded with its trade's records: welding records, NDT and traceability for steel; tickets, inspections and cube results for concrete. Heavy items drill into real registers — one row per pour, with tickets, pre- and post-pour inspections and cube strengths tracked against the same rows — and their percentages go automatic: complete over applicable, no drifting spreadsheet totals. Four states everywhere: complete, in progress, not started, N/A.

THE OTHER HALF OF THE MONEY

What you're owed, what's at risk, and the application on the letterhead.

Contract sum, variations that only count once instructed, applications against certificates against payments, retention held. Two numbers on the dashboard every morning: OWED — certified and not paid — and AT RISK — applied and not certified, the undervaluation most subbies never see written down. The application for payment generates itself from the registers, with standard VAT or the domestic reverse charge wording printed correctly. And upstream of all of it: a Tender list — enquiries priced, submitted, won or lost with a reason — where WON becomes a live job in one click.

JOB-010SITE MODE · MAJOR PROJECTS
THE RIGHT VIEW FOR THE RIGHT PERSON

Site sees the work. The PM sees the money.

Switch a phone into Site Mode and it becomes a site manager's tool: parts, drawings, 3D, programme, snags, handover, meetings — editable, nothing else. No money, no AI briefs, no subcontractor position, no tender list. A PIN brings PM mode back. Same permission table becomes the server-enforced roles when team accounts land.

And when the job is a major project rather than a shed: flip one switch and areas become sections — Building 2, Phase 3 — running through parts, programme, snags, handover groups, meetings and subcontractor allocations, so a section-by-section board tells the major-projects PM which part of the job is bleeding.

UK CONSTRUCTIONCOMPLIANCE

Paperwork as a by-product.

No law says "use software." UK construction law and your clients' contracts mandate records — traceable, current, contemporaneous. GRIDLINE keeps them as the exhaust of running the job.

EN 1090 / UKCA

Factory Production Control traceability — which steel, to which mark, to which drawing revision. The register is the audit evidence.

Construction Act payments

Assessed progress, PMIs, paid and due — the contemporaneous record the HGCRA notice regime runs on, from both sides.

NEC / JCT records

A living programme, a numbered instruction register, dated snags with photos — the evidence that decides compensation events. Adjudicators believe the party with the records.

CDM 2015

Drawings under revision control and a snag-and-photo trail feed the health & safety file without a scanning weekend.

ISO 19650

Managed, revision-controlled information — the answer when a tier-one asks how you control your drawings.

Prequals

ISO 9001, Constructionline, SSIP — demonstrated document control and traceability, off the shelf, because it's how the jobs already run.
