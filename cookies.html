Cookies & Device Storage

What GRIDLINE keeps in your browser, and why there is no cookie banner.

The short version
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising cookies — on this website or in the app.
  • This website sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.
  • The app keeps only what it needs to work, which is why there is no cookie banner.

1. What the app keeps on your device

The app uses your browser’s own storage rather than cookies. Everything in it is there because you are using the service:

WhatWhy
Your sign-in sessionKeeps you signed in.
A copy of your jobs, photos and drawingsSo the app is fast and keeps working without signal on site.
Changes waiting to syncSo work done without signal isn’t lost before it reaches the server.
Your settingsTheme, device name, and the Site Mode PIN.
Today’s “seen” marks and cached briefsSo the Today list and AI briefs don’t reset every time you open the app.
Your Anthropic API key — only if you enter oneSo the AI features can run. Kept in this browser only; never sent to us.
A device pairing — only if you pair a signerSo you don’t have to pair your signing device every time.

2. Why there is no banner

The law lets a service store what is strictly necessary to provide something you asked for without asking consent first. Everything above falls into that category. If we ever want to store anything that doesn’t, we will ask first.

3. Clearing it

Signing out clears everything above from that device, except your choice of theme. Deleting your account does the same. You can also clear it at any time through your browser’s settings.

4. Third parties

No third party sets cookies through GRIDLINE. Our fonts are served from our own pages. The services listed in the privacy policy see your IP address when the app talks to them, but don’t place cookies through us.
