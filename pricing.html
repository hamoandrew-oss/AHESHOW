Questions

Good to know

Is my data safe?

Accounts and jobs are stored in a database hosted in London, with access controlled row by row so only your project's members can read them. No tracking and no advertising. AI features only run when you press the button, under your own Anthropic key. Download a full backup any time; delete your account from Settings. See the privacy policy.

Why is it free right now?

Charging means accounts, billing and support obligations, and we'd rather finish those properly. The solo app is complete and free; the pilot bridges the gap; pricing starts when team accounts are real.

Why £129?

It's under £1,600 a year: roughly one wasted trip for missing steel, one re-typed handover weekend, or one payment argument you couldn't evidence. Flat pricing means the whole team is on it, which is the only way a live register works.

Does it work with ACC?

Today: import ACC checksheet (form) reports, RFI exports (ACC or BIM 360) and the files log for red lines. A live ACC connection is in development.
