THE MATHSWHY £129

Priced against one mistake.

£129 a month is under £1,600 a year — roughly one wasted trip to site for missing steel, one re-typed handover weekend, or one payment argument you couldn't evidence. The register that prevents any single one of those has paid for the year. Per-seat tools punish you for putting the fitters on the system; flat pricing means the whole crew is on it, which is the only way a live register works.

Procore-class platforms — £4,000+ a year and a sales call to find that out. Built for tier ones, priced like it.
Field apps per-seat — £25–60 per user per month. Ten people on site and you're at £5k/yr before the office logs in.
GRIDLINE — £129 a month, everyone on, built by a contractor who erects frames for a living. The paperwork becomes the by-product.
Is my data safe?

Today, in solo mode, your data lives in your own browser — it never touches our servers, which is as private as it gets. Team accounts add a secured, UK/EU-hosted database with server-enforced roles, and your data exports with one click whenever you want out. We're contractors: we wouldn't put our own subcontractor payment records anywhere we didn't trust, and ours run on this.

Why is the app free right now?

Because charging money means accounts, billing and support obligations, and we'd rather finish those properly than fake them. The solo app is complete and genuinely free; the pilot bridges the gap; pricing starts when team accounts are real.

What does the ACC integration do?

Today: import your Form detail report and 451 checksheets land in the handover file in one click, statuses updating on every re-import. Coming: live connection — drawings revisions, RFIs, and forms syncing without the export step.

What happens if you disappear?

Fair question to ask any small vendor. Your data is exportable at any time, and the solo app runs entirely in your browser without our servers even existing. You are never locked in and never locked out.

We're bigger than 15 people — does it still fit?

Probably, and the flat price gets more comical the bigger your crew. If you're a main contractor wanting your supply chain on it, that's the third column — talk to us.
