Refunds & Cancellation

What happens if you cancel, and when you get money back.

Right now
  • GRIDLINE is free during the pilot, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Nothing is ever charged automatically — not at the end of the pilot, not ever. You choose whether to subscribe.

The rest of this page sets out how cancellation and refunds will work once paid plans start, so you know before you ever pay.

1. Monthly plans

Cancel at any time by emailing hamo.andrew@gmail.com or from your account once that option exists. Your plan runs to the end of the month you have paid for, and you are not charged again. We don’t refund part-months, except where the law requires it or where we failed to provide the service.

2. Annual plans

If you cancel within 14 days of paying, we refund you in full. After that, cancelling stops the plan renewing and it runs to the end of the year you paid for.

3. If you are a consumer

If you are buying as an individual rather than for a business, you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of signing up. If you ask us to start the service straight away and then cancel within those 14 days, we may keep an amount for the time you used it. If GRIDLINE is not provided with reasonable care and skill, you have rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, which this policy does not affect.

4. If we stop the service

If we stop providing GRIDLINE, or a plan you have paid for, we refund the unused part of any period you paid in advance, and give you the chance to download your data first.

5. Your data when you cancel

Cancelling never locks you out of your own data. You can download a full backup at any time from Settings → Backup, and delete your account whenever you choose.

6. How to ask

Email hamo.andrew@gmail.com with the email address on your account. We confirm within five working days, and refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of us agreeing them.
