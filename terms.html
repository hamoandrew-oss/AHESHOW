5. Contract clocks and deadlines

GRIDLINE works out NEC4 periods, such as clauses 61.3 and 62.6, from the dates you enter and the standard periods in the published contract. Please read this carefully:

  • It is arithmetic, not legal advice. It does not read your actual contract.
  • Amendments, Z clauses, agreed extensions and the other party’s own position can all change the real period. GRIDLINE does not know about them unless you enter them.
  • Dates GRIDLINE had to assume are shown as assumed and raise no deadline until you confirm them.
  • Serving notices, and serving them on time, remains your responsibility. Don’t rely on GRIDLINE as your only reminder, and check the periods against your contract.
